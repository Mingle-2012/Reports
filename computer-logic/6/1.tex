\section*{Assignment 6}

\subsection*{1}

Check whether the following model satisfies the CTL formulas, provide the set of states that satisfies each formula
\begin{itemize}
    \item AG(Start→EF Heat)
\end{itemize}

$AG(Start \to EF \, Heat) = \neg E[\top U (start \wedge \neg EF \, Heat)]$

$S(M \vDash Heat) = \{4,7\}$

$S(M \vDash EF \, Heat) = \{1,2,3,4,5,6,7\}$

$S(M \vDash \neg EF \, Heat) = \emptyset$

$S(M \vDash (start \wedge \neg EF \, Heat)) = \emptyset$

$S(M \vDash \neg E[\top U (start \wedge \neg EF \, Heat)]) = \{1,2,3,4,5,6,7\}$

Yes, the model satisfies the CTL formulas.

\subsection*{2}

\begin{itemize}
    \item AG(A[$\neg$Error U (EF Close)])
\end{itemize}

$AG(A[\neg Error \, U \, (EF \, Close)]) = \neg E[\top U (E[\neg EF \, Close \, U \, (Error \wedge \neg EF \, Close)] \vee \neg AF(EF \, Close))]$

$S(M \vDash Close) = \{3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$

$S(M \vDash EF \, Close) = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$

$S(M \vDash \neg EF \, Close) = \emptyset$

$S(M \vDash \neg AF(EF \, Close)) = \emptyset$

$S(M \vDash Error \wedge \neg EF \, Close) = \emptyset$

$S(M \vDash E[\neg EF \, Close \, U \, (Error \wedge \neg EF \, Close)]) = \emptyset$

$S(M \vDash (E[\neg EF \, Close \, U \, (Error \wedge \neg EF \, Close)] \vee \neg AF(EF \, Close))) = \emptyset$

$S(M \vDash \neg E[\top U (E[\neg EF \, Close \, U \, (Error \wedge \neg EF \, Close)] \vee \neg AF(EF \, Close))]) = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$

Yes, the model satisfies the CTL formulas.
